Integration

Live in three steps

No infrastructure to provision. No data to prepare. Works with any HTTP client.

1

Get a free API key

One POST /v1/keys call with your name and email. No credit card. Your key arrives in the response — prefix st_.

2

POST your query

Call /v1/search with a natural language query. Optionally scope to a tradition, language, or specific corpus code. Results in under 100ms.

3

Receive ranked passages

Every result includes the passage text, canonical citation (e.g. 2:255, SN 56.11), tradition, language, and relevance score.
