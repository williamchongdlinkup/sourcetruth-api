2.5 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store the raw API key — only its SHA-256 hash

  • We do not store the content of /v1/answer responses — generated answer text is returned to you and not retained by us
  • We do not link anonymous usage analytics to your account or API key

  • Security and abuse prevention: Detect and prevent misuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • Legal compliance: Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Anonymous usage analytics (api_usage table): Retained indefinitely. This data is not personal data and is not subject to deletion obligations under data protection law.
  • Customer Intelligence Programme data (linked usage, opted-in customers): Retained while the customer remains enrolled. On opt-out, retained for 90 days then anonymised. On deletion request to api@sourcetruth.io, deleted within 30 days. Anonymisation of historic data does not affect models or benchmarks already produced from that data.
  • Daily quota counts: 2 days rolling
  • API key records (api_keys table): Retained while the key is active. Deleted within 30 days of account termination on request.
  • Email addresses: Retained while the account is active. On request to delete, we remove the email from the api_keys record within 30 days. Note that Stripe may retain email data subject to its own retention policies.
  • Stripe billing data: Retained by Stripe per their data retention policy (typically 7 years for financial records).

6. Data Security

  • API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes — the raw key is never stored and cannot be recovered
  • All API traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS (Cloudflare-managed SSL)
  • Database connections use TLS in transit
  • We use Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies to restrict data access
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel and service accounts

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and relevant regulators as required by applicable law.

7.1 Singapore PDPA

We are subject to the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore) ("PDPA"). You have the right to access and correct your personal data held by us. To make a request, email us at api@sourcetruth.io with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. If you believe we have not handled your personal data in accordance with the PDPA, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) at www.pdpc.gov.sg.

7.2 GDPR (EEA/UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) / UK GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance: account data (name, email) collected and used to provide the Service
  • Legitimate interests: quota enforcement, security, and abuse prevention
  • Not applicable: anonymous usage analytics (§2.2) are not personal data — GDPR does not apply to them
  • Consent: Customer Intelligence Programme data (§2.3) — enrolment constitutes explicit, informed, freely given consent to the processing described in Terms of Service §8.3

Data transfers to the United States rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses with our sub-processors. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
