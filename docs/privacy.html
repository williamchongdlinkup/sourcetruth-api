D'Linkup Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") operates the SourceTruth API ("Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose information from and about you when you use the Service.

2.5 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store the raw API key — only its SHA-256 hash

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Service operation: Authenticate API keys, enforce rate limits and quotas, process billing
  • Communication: Send transactional emails (billing receipts, account notices, service changes) to the email on your API key record
  • Usage analytics and product improvement: Usage data (§2.2) is used to improve the Service, expand the corpus, and improve retrieval quality
  • Internal AI model training: Anonymous usage data (§2.2) is used to train and evaluate our own internal retrieval and ranking models. See §3.1 for detail.
  • Customer Intelligence Programme: Where you have opted in (§2.3), linked usage data is used for the purposes described in Terms of Service §8.3: usage dashboards, corpus recommendations, retrieval tuning, customer success, internal product analytics, model training, and aggregated benchmarking.
  • Security and abuse prevention: Detect and prevent misuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • Legal compliance: Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3.1 AI Model Training — Scope and Limits

When we refer to "internal AI model training" as a purpose for usage data, this means:

  • Training and improving our own internal retrieval and ranking models to improve the quality of /v1/search results
  • Evaluating retrieval performance against benchmarks derived from real-world query patterns
  • Improving prompt design for /v1/answer generation

We do not sell, licence, or share query data with third parties for their model training purposes. Anthropic processes query text in real time as part of /v1/answer request fulfilment (inference only) — we do not send query data to Anthropic for fine-tuning. See §4 for Anthropic's role as sub-processor.

3.2 AI Training and the Right to Deletion

Where query data has been used to train or evaluate internal AI models prior to a deletion request, deleting the source data does not automatically alter trained model weights already produced. This is a technical limitation of current AI training methods. We will suppress the use of any identified personal data in future training cycles and, where technically feasible, will retrain or adjust models to remove the influence of data subject to verified deletion requests. We disclose this limitation rather than making an unqualified deletion promise.

5. Data Retention

  • Anonymous usage analytics (api_usage table): Retained indefinitely as non-personal data. Where a specific query is identified as containing personal data, it may be deleted on request per the process in §2.2.
  • Customer Intelligence Programme data (linked usage, opted-in customers): Retained while the customer remains enrolled. On opt-out, retained for 90 days then anonymised. On deletion request to api@sourcetruth.io, deleted within 30 days. Anonymisation of historic data does not affect models or benchmarks already produced — see §3.2.
  • Daily quota counts: 2 days rolling
  • API key records (api_keys table): Retained while the key is active. Deleted within 30 days of account termination on request.
  • Email addresses: Retained while the account is active. On request to delete, we remove the email from the api_keys record within 30 days. Note that Stripe may retain email data subject to its own retention policies (typically 7 years for financial records).
  • Stripe billing data: Retained by Stripe per their data retention policy.

6. Data Security

  • API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes — the raw key is never stored and cannot be recovered
  • All API traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS (Cloudflare-managed SSL)
  • Database connections use TLS in transit
  • We use Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) policies to restrict data access
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel and service accounts

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will comply with our mandatory notification obligations: under Singapore's PDPA (as amended 2021) we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) and affected individuals within 3 calendar days of becoming aware of a notifiable breach; under the GDPR we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware. We will contact you at the email address on your API key record.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate data be corrected
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements and the limitation described in §3.2)
  • Portability: Request your personal data in a machine-readable format (CSV or JSON). We will fulfil verified portability requests within 30 days. Portability applies to data you actively provided (name, email address); it does not apply to anonymous usage analytics.
  • Objection / restriction: Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent to non-essential processing at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at api@sourcetruth.io with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

7.2 GDPR (EEA/UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) / UK GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance (Article 6(1)(b)): Name and email address collected and used to provide the Service and process billing
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Quota enforcement, security, and abuse prevention. Our legitimate interests assessment considers that these activities are necessary for the integrity of the Service and that the data processed (daily request counts, API key hashes) is minimal and technical in nature, and does not override the fundamental interests or rights of data subjects.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Customer Intelligence Programme data (§2.3) — enrolment constitutes explicit, informed, freely given consent to the processing described in Terms of Service §8.3
  • Not applicable: Anonymous usage analytics (§2.2) that do not constitute personal data are not subject to GDPR. Where any query text constitutes personal data, we rely on legitimate interests for its processing given the minimal nature of the data and the non-identifiable way it is stored.

Data transfers to the United States are made only to sub-processors that have committed to appropriate data protection safeguards: for EEA transfers, under GDPR Chapter V including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the sub-processor is certified) and Standard Contractual Clauses (2021 EC SCCs); for UK transfers, under UK GDPR Article 46 including the UK-US Data Bridge (where certified) and UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) or UK Addendum SCCs. The applicable transfer mechanism for each sub-processor is available on request at api@sourcetruth.io.

If you are located in the EEA and believe we have not handled your personal data in accordance with the GDPR, you may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your EU member state. If you are located in the United Kingdom, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
